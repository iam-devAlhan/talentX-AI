This document says about fake internships offerings:

Fake internships are mostly remote offered by some companies on Linkedin, The following red flags are:

- They ask you for money as processing fee for certification completion / permanent job placement.
- They are mostly remote based.
- They are mostly unpaid disguised as paid internship.
- They promise for mentorship but 0% mentorship and want labour for free.
- Mostly scam companies don't have website in their Linkedin or they do their hiring on WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram and many social platforms.
- They promise that they will help you engage with professionals and learning based internship but there is no learning from day one, only tasks piled up for you.
- Meaningless and worthless project tasks like Todo App, Simple Dashboard.
- They are mostly in Linkedin and some in Indeed, Glassdoor and other job platforms.
